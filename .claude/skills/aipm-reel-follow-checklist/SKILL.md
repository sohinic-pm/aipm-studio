# AI Product Simplified: follow-conversion checklist

Goal: reels that earn follows, not just likes. Baseline (30 days to 2026-11-06): 3,902 viewers, 51 profile visits (1.3%), 20 followers (0.5% of viewers). The leak is between a like and a profile visit.

## Before scripting
1. Read the project's instagram-playbook file first, plus the end-card rules in brand-end-card.md. Those rules (first frame, 15-20s length, pattern interrupts at ~5s and ~12s, share line at ~10-12s, PM takeaway) still apply.
2. Audience: 18-34, India-heavy, mostly men; the target slice is early-career PMs and job seekers aged 25-34.

## Script must include all of these
1. **Promise early:** one line at the ~10-12s beat, next to the share line, saying who this is for ("this is for PMs who..."). It names the audience only and never names the next topic.
2. **Signature moment:** one recurring named on-screen moment at the same beat in every reel (for example a "PM call:" line giving the cost, risk or ship decision). On-screen text only, no mascot.
3. **Author line:** one first-person line from lived PM work (for example "when I wrote tickets, I'd..."). Use method only. Never use confidential employer details, names or data.
4. **Voice:** prefer Sohini's own recorded voice (6-7 short lines with ~1s gaps); use TTS only if she can't record.
5. **Proof:** prefer real, privacy-edited product footage over "illustrative, recreated" mockups. Blur everything except the selected element, and scan every frame for leaks.
6. **Freebie:** if there is a keyword freebie, make it follower-gated ("Follow, then comment KEYWORD"). One keyword per post, matching the caption and pinned comment.

## End card
Follow brand-end-card.md exactly: fixed brand block, one call to action in the CTA slot, no teaser, no emoji, no mascot, no reel-specific theme. Gold #FFD36B, Inter, #0B0D12 background.

## Before posting, confirm
- First frame: one complete high-contrast line of 8 words or fewer, with a number, named company or coined term.
- Promise line, signature moment and author line are all present.
- News facts are verified; illustrations are labelled.

## 24h review (log for every reel)
Skip rate, avg watch, share rate, saves, **profile visits, follows**. Pass targets: skip rate 50% or lower, avg watch 35% of length or more, share rate above 0%. Report whether profile visits per 1,000 viewers beat the 13 baseline.

## First test
Fix 1 (promise early) on Ep 5, judged on profile visits and follows at 24h.
