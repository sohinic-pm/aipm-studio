## 20 Nov 2026: 30-day audit (22 Oct to 19 Nov, 23 posts)
- **Reach comes from big-company AI news with money at stake.** Tokenmaxxing
  (2,050) and the market drop (613) brought 47% of all reel views. Median reel: 205.
- **Depth comes from practical PM content.** Interactions per viewer: Toolkit Ep 2
  7.6%, Ep 3 7.0%, Ep 1 3.9%, against 0.8% for Tokenmaxxing. 3 of the 6 saves were
  PM content.
- **Carousels are invisible at this size.** 5 carousels, 23 average views, about 4%
  from non-followers, 0 saves.
- **Strangers watch but don't act.** On 9 of 11 reels with the split, 70-100% of
  interactions came from existing followers. 0 follows credited to any post.
- **A "why" promise holds attention.** "But why?" and "Here's why" openers beat reels
  that just state the fact.
- **India angles are unproven.** Voice scam (205) mid-pack. Indian-language token cost
  (129) went out in week 1, before the account had history. Keep testing.
- **Following the format rules isn't enough.** Ep 3 followed every rule and got 128.
