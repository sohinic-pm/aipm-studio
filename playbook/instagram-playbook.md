# Instagram playbook: aipm.decoded

Source of truth for what we make and why. Last revised 20 Nov 2026, after the
30-day audit (see `docs/decisions-log.md`).

## The product in one line
AI news turned into a PM lesson for freshers, from a PM who has done the work.

## Pillars and the job each one does
| Pillar | Job | Evidence (22 Oct to 17 Nov) |
|---|---|---|
| AI news and AI fails (big companies) | Reach | Median 259 views; only pillar to break out (2,050 and 613) |
| PM career, PM Toolkit, interview questions | Depth and follows | Median 157 views, but highest interactions per viewer |
| AI concept explainers | Steady reach | Median 202 views, little action |

**Join the pillars.** Every news reel opens with the news hook and ends on a
PM lesson for a fresher. Every PM reel bridges from a big name where it can:
"Amazon tracked AI use. Your PM interviewer will ask about it."

## Formats
- **Reels only** until the account passes about 300 followers. Carousels reach
  almost only existing followers (average 23 views, about 4% non-followers).
- Carousel ideas become reels, the depth goes into the Notion freebie, and
  multi-frame follow-ups go into stories (2-3 frames + a poll).
- Length 15-20 seconds before the end card. Pattern interrupts at about 5s and
  12s. Share line and who-it's-for line at 10-12s.
- On-screen captions for the voiceover on every reel.

## Hook rules (first frame)
1. 8 words or fewer, one complete high-contrast line.
2. Contains a number, a named company or a coined term.
3. **Novelty beats clarity.** A new term or a fresh consequence wins. A story
   people already know (glue on pizza, Air Canada) loses even with a perfect frame.
4. A data card is fine if it has 3 rows or fewer and reads at thumbnail size.
5. No brand name, no "FREE", no series label in frame 1. Outcome first,
   freebie on the end card.
6. No insider jargon a fresher wouldn't know (Jira ticket, backlog grooming).

Formulas from our own winners: see `hook-formulas.md`.

## Every reel must have
- Who-it's-for line at 10-12s ("This is for anyone breaking into PM...")
- A `[FIRST-PERSON]` line from Sohini's PM work (method only)
- The signature "PM call:" moment (cost, risk or ship decision)
- CTA: "Follow, then comment START". One keyword across the page.
- Standard end card from `brand-end-card.md`

## Cadence
- 5 reels a week, about 6:30-7:30 PM IST
- **Every Thursday: a PM interview question** (stated in the bio)
- Sunday: no post, 24-hour reviews and next week's plan

## Fact rules
Every news fact and figure is checked against a primary or reputable source
before posting. If two sources disagree, use the more conservative number and
say "reported".
