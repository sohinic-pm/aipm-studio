## How to fill this in
- Handle: the Instagram @handle, no link needed.
- Follower count: as of the date you added/updated the row (add the date in Notes
  if it's not from today).
- Niche overlap: what they actually cover (e.g. "AI PM news", "interview prep",
  "career switch stories") — not every page in the niche covers the same angle.
- What to learn from it: free text — format, cadence, or what's distinctive.
